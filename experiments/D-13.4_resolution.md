# D-13.4 — where step-6 tuning happens

**Status: RESOLVED 2026-08-10 (Phase 13h).** Paste into plan.md §4's decision table.

**Decision.** Tuning happens on **`dev_v1`**, a separately annotated slice drawn from
**outside** the frozen `gt_v1` scored set. Neither of the fallbacks was taken: `gt_v1` is not
swept, and no tuned number is reported as an upper bound.

**Why not the alternatives.**

- *Hold a dev slice out of `gt_v1`* — rejected on arithmetic. Only 102 of the 199 scored
  images carry text, and four strata have three or fewer text-bearing images (`us_other` 1,
  `us_sonosite` 2, `ct_axial` 2, `ct_secondary_capture` 3). A holdout that spans strata and
  vendors therefore either deletes a stratum from the scored set or leaves it untuned, and it
  shrinks the headline set by roughly a fifth on a set that is already small for per-stratum
  reporting.
- *Tune on synthetic fixtures only* — rejected as unmeasuring. The fixtures are drawn text on
  clean backgrounds, and the knob that matters most here (`text_det_limit_side_len`: small
  burned-in tokens shrunk below detector resolution) plus EasyOCR's `low_text` on faint
  ultrasound overlays are exactly what synthetic does not reproduce. It would pick defaults or
  pick wrong, with no way to tell which without the real set.
- *Report the tuned number as an upper bound with the sweep size disclosed* — viable, and the
  cheapest, but it costs the claim: the tuned row could then be quoted only as a bound, never
  as a measurement, and the stock-vs-tuned delta would carry an unknown inflation.

**Cost accepted.** ~26 images / ~515 boxes of annotation (about a fifth of `gt_v1`'s 2,351),
and a human step between the code and the sweep.

**Rules this decision creates.**

1. `dev_v1` is its own artifact with its own hashes (`dev_v1.csv.sha256`,
   `dev_v1_set.sha256`) and its own summary. It is **never** merged into `gt.csv`, and
   `gt_v1`'s hashes are unchanged by its existence.
2. The sweep **refuses** to run against the scored set, by path and by content hash, with no
   override flag.
3. A tuned config, once chosen on `dev_v1`, is **frozen** into
   `experiments/tuned_configs.json` before the `gt_v1` run. Re-tuning after seeing `gt_v1`
   numbers reintroduces the exact bias the slice was annotated to avoid.
4. A candidate must beat stock on the pooled ranking metric **and** regress no single stratum
   by more than the disclosed tolerance. "Nothing beat stock" is a valid, reportable outcome;
   the stock arm then stands and `<engine>:tuned` stays unbuildable.
5. The sweep size is recorded per engine regardless, and the ranking metric is named in the
   report — so what "tuned" was tuned for is never in doubt.

**Sweep budget.** Equal per engine and disclosed in the report: docTR 5 configs, PP-OCRv6 6,
EasyOCR 2 (its pair is fixed by definition — library defaults 0.7/0.4 versus the 0.2/0.2
config Phase 9 shipped — so it is not swept at all).
